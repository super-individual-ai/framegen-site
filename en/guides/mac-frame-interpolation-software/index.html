Which Mac apps do frame interpolation and upscaling?

Updated November 25, 2026 · OpenDuckFrame 0.6.2 · 中文版

Start with whether you want to watch in real time or export a file. To enhance a browser, game window or whole display in real time, try OpenDuckFrame, a free open-source Mac app. For smoother playback inside a player, use SVP with IINA or mpv. To export processed video, use Topaz Video or LocalSR, or optical-flow retiming in DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro. Lossless Scaling and Flowframes have no official Mac version. Facts below were checked against each vendor's official pages on November 25, 2026. This is not a performance test.

Which one fits your use

  • Real-time enhancement of browser video, a game window or the whole screen: try OpenDuckFrame. It processes pictures that are already displayed and does not change a game's own rendering rate. Interpolation adds latency, so check input feel in games.
  • Smoother local movies inside a player: SVP with IINA or mpv is the established option. For a free option that can also export, look at Stellaria Motion.
  • Export a processed video file: Topaz Video (subscription) for quality, LocalSR as a free open-source option, or optical-flow retiming in DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro for footage you are already editing.
  • Upscale still images only: Upscayl.
  • Repeatable batch processing from the command line: FFmpeg or rife-ncnn-vulkan.
  • Coming from Lossless Scaling on Windows: it has no official Mac version. See this comparison for similar uses on a Mac.

OpenDuckFrame requirements and limits

  • Needs an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later. Apple low-latency interpolation and Apple super resolution need supported macOS 26 devices; Apple super resolution needs 8-bit SDR input, and live Apple video super resolution requires frame generation to be off.
  • The ODF-VFI engine is reduced from RIFE 4.25. The fast tier is validated; balanced and hq are still experimental.
  • Window and display capture need Screen Recording permission, and protected content may not be capturable. Audio still plays from the source app and can drift from the picture.
  • It does not export processed video files. A multiplier is a requested mode; actual frame rate depends on the source, device and display refresh rate.
  • The current community build is not Apple notarized. Check the download source before allowing the first launch.
